Orbit Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Orbit is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Orbit collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

When you create an Orbit account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • User ID (UID)

When using Orbit, we may also collect:

  • Chat messages
  • Reports submitted by users
  • Basic usage and diagnostic information

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate Orbit
  • Match users for conversations
  • Moderate content and investigate reports
  • Improve the service
  • Protect users and enforce our policies

Chat Messages

Chat messages may be stored temporarily or permanently for moderation, abuse prevention, safety investigations, and service operation.

Users should not assume that conversations are private or permanent.

Reports

When a user submits a report, Orbit may store:

  • Report details
  • Chat history related to the report
  • User identifiers involved in the conversation

This information is used solely for moderation and safety purposes.

Information Sharing

Orbit does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when required by law or when necessary to protect the safety of users, Orbit, or the public.

Data Security

Orbit uses commercially reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Orbit is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Users under the minimum age required by applicable law may not use Orbit.

Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion through features provided within the app or by contacting Orbit support.

Changes to This Policy

Orbit may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of any updated policy.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Orbit team through official support channels.
