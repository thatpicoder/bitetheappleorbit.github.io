Orbit Community Guidelines

Last Updated: June 2026

Orbit exists to help people have fun, respectful conversations with others around the world. By using Orbit, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

Be Respectful

Treat other users with kindness and respect. Disagreements are allowed, but harassment, bullying, threats, and hate speech are not.

No Sexual Content

Sexual content is not allowed on Orbit.

  • Do not request or send nude images.
  • Do not engage in sexually explicit conversations.
  • Do not ask users for sexual content.

Protect Personal Information

Do not share personal information with strangers.

  • Addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • School information
  • Passwords
  • Financial information
  • Other sensitive personal details

No Harassment or Abuse

The following behavior is prohibited:

  • Harassment
  • Threats
  • Bullying
  • Stalking
  • Intimidation
  • Discriminatory behavior

No Illegal Activity

Do not use Orbit to plan, encourage, or participate in illegal activity.

No Spam or Scams

  • Do not send spam.
  • Do not advertise scams.
  • Do not impersonate other people.
  • Do not attempt to trick users into revealing personal information.

Reporting Violations

If you encounter behavior that violates these guidelines, use Orbit's reporting tools. Reports help us keep the community safe.

Enforcement

Violations of these guidelines may result in warnings, temporary restrictions, account suspension, or permanent bans.

Help Keep Orbit Safe

Every user plays a role in creating a welcoming community. Be respectful, report harmful behavior, and help make Orbit a positive place for everyone.
