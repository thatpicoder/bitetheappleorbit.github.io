About Orbit

Orbit is a random chat platform that connects people from around the world through simple, real-time conversations.

Our goal is to make meeting new people easy, safe, and enjoyable.

What is Orbit?

Orbit matches users with other online users for one-on-one text conversations. No friend requests, no followers, and no complicated setup—just chat.

Our Mission

We believe the internet should make it easier for people to connect. Orbit is designed to encourage genuine conversations while prioritizing user safety and privacy.

Safety First

Orbit includes moderation systems, reporting tools, account management, and community guidelines to help maintain a safe environment for users.

Built by bitetheapple

Orbit is independently developed by bitetheapple.

What began as a small project has grown into a platform focused on creating meaningful conversations between people worldwide.

Current Status

Orbit is currently in beta. Features, policies, and functionality may change as the platform continues to grow and improve.
